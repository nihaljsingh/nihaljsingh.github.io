Piehouse Co-Op

March 2025


In collaboration with Jonathan Blower, we designed and built a custom mixing and sound desk for Piehouse Co-Op, a music venue in Deptford. Using reclaimed materials from Peckham, the pieces were made to fit the tight space while keeping sustainability and functionality at the forefront.

The mixing desk was built higher than standard, with a raised platform, so technicians could see over the audience and hear the music clearly to make live adjustments.

I also modified the bar by lowering part of it to create a wheelchair-accessible section.
